About

Background

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, working under the supervision of Dr. Vibhav Bharadwaj Shivakumar.

My research combines finite-element simulation, femtosecond laser fabrication and hands-on instrument building. I model stress-induced waveguides and plasmonic structures in COMSOL, fabricate devices in diamond, and build the optical and microwave setups that read out NV spin states.

I am a fellow on the PARIMANA project, supported by the QMET Fellowship, which is developing an integrated diamond photonic platform for quantum imaging and biosensing.

News and highlights

  • Sep 2026

    Diamond magnetometers record the human heartbeat

    A study in Science Advances reports non-invasive, non-contact magnetocardiography (MCG) on humans using NV-center diamond sensors built by three independent teams: JGU Mainz, the University of Stuttgart and Q.ANT. The room-temperature sensors reach 6–26 pT/√Hz with sensing volumes below 0.5 mm³, and measurements were made in both shielded and unshielded settings. The work is a step toward compact quantum sensors for clinical heart and brain diagnostics, and it is closely related to my own work on NV magnetometer probes.

    Omar et al., “Human cardiac measurements with diamond magnetometers,” Science Advances (2026). Read the paper →

Research

Research interests

Laser-written diamond photonics

Type II stress waveguides written into diamond with femtosecond pulses. I model the stress field in full 3D and convert it to refractive-index change through the piezo-optic tensor, then design Y-splitters and directional couplers for on-chip NV light at 637 nm.

fs-laser writingCOMSOLBPM

NV magnetometry and biosensing

Optically detected magnetic resonance (ODMR) with NV centers: waveguide-based line-scan sensing, fiber-tip and pen-type probes, and T₁ relaxometry for biomarker detection.

ODMRT₁ relaxometryfiber probes

Plasmonic enhancement of NV emission

Gold nanosphere and nanorod hybrids with nanodiamonds. I tune the localized surface plasmon resonance to the NV emission band, compute Purcell factors with orientation-averaged dipole studies, and check the models against Mie theory.

LSPRPurcell factorMie theory

Nanodiamond spectroscopy and ML

Photoluminescence of nanodiamonds at room temperature and 77 K, surface treatment and annealing studies, and Z-scan nonlinear optics. I also work on machine-learning models for ODMR data and the gap between simulated and real spectra.
